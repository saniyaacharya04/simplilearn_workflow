SIMPLILEARN OSL 3.0 INSTRUCTIONAL STORYBOARDING GUIDELINES
Derived Strictly from guidelines.md (OSL 3.0, Learning Design & CE Standards)
Accounting for Subject Matter Expert (SME) Positioning & Numerical Clearances
================================================================================

1. CORE LEARNING DESIGN PHILOSOPHY (guidelines.md Section 1)
Every visual element and storyboarding decision exists to enforce LEARNING CLARITY.
- Outcome-Driven: Every on-screen element must directly support a clear learning outcome.
- Audio is Primary (Rule 1): The SME audio is the instructional source of truth.
  Listen to the audio first without visuals; surface and reinforce the structure
  already present. Never invent visual structure that contradicts or distracts from SME audio.
- Eliminate Split-Attention (Rule 2 & Section 3.2): Learners must NEVER split attention
  between reading on-screen paragraphs and listening to narration. On-screen text (OST)
  must distill spoken sentences into short keywords, steps, or anchors. NEVER duplicate
  spoken sentences verbatim.
- Relevancy Test (Rule 4): For every visual asset, answer: "Where does the SME talk
  about this?" If the SME never refers to it, remove it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PEDAGOGICAL LESSON PROGRESSION (guidelines.md Section 2.1)
Every topic segment (ideal length 6–10 minutes) must follow the guided narrative arc:
context → teaching → engagement → recap

Step 1: HOOK / INTRODUCTION
- Purpose: Grabs attention, establishes real-world professional context.
- SME Layout: 100% Full-Screen SME (Canvas 1920x1080).
- OST: Slide title (5–7 words, Chicago Title Casing) and core thematic anchor phrase.

Step 2: LEARNING OBJECTIVES
- Purpose: Tells the learner what to expect and sets measurable goals.
- SME Layout: 70% Left Rounded-Rectangle SME.
- OST: Right-side workspace (x >= 760 px).
- Lead-In Line: "By the end of this topic, you will be able to:"
- Cards: 3 to 4 concise bullet cards starting with action verbs (e.g., "Build...", "Analyze...").

Step 3: CONCEPT EXPLANATION
- Purpose: Delivers core theoretical foundation, architectures, and principles.
- SME Layout: 20% Top-Right Corner Picture-In-Picture (PIP) SME.
- OST: Full slide visual workspace (staying clear of top-right exclusion zone).

Step 4: EXAMPLE OR DEMONSTRATION
- Purpose: Shows the concept in action through workflow walkthroughs or code demos.
- SME Layout: 20% Top-Right Corner PIP SME.
- Pacing: Progressive reveal of stages. Annotate key steps; zoom into details smoothly.

Step 5: LEARNER ACTIVITY / PAUSE PROMPT (Active Learning - Section 1.2 & 2.1)
- Purpose: Prompts learners to reflect, predict, or try an exercise before resuming.
- SME Layout: 70% Left Rounded-Rectangle SME or Centered Prompt Card.
- OST: Clear challenge question or reflection prompt (e.g., "Pause & Reflect: How would you index this dataset?").

Step 6: SUMMARY & KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Purpose: Synthesizes and reinforces the dominant principles.
- SME Layout: 70% Left Rounded-Rectangle SME.
- OST: Right-side workspace with 2–3 high-retention summary cards.

Step 7: WHAT'S NEXT (Consistent Video Closing - Section 4.4)
- Purpose: Seamless bridge to the next topic in the course sequence.
- SME Layout: 70% Left Rounded-Rectangle SME.
- Mandatory Closing Syntax: Must strictly use the exact phrasing:
  "In the next topic, you will learn about: <Action in -ing>"
  (e.g., "In the next topic, you will learn about: Evaluating model accuracy with cross-validation.")

3. SME POSITIONING & EXACT NUMERICAL CLEARANCE SPECIFICATIONS (guidelines.md Section 8)
All visual staging operates on a 1920x1080 (16:9 Full HD) canvas (1 Manim unit = 135 px).
Because recordings are captured in natural environments without green screen or AI matting,
all graphics, cards, and text MUST strictly obey the spatial boundaries of the SME:

A. LAYOUT 1: SME 100% — FULL-SCREEN PRESENTER (guidelines.md Section 8.3)
- Center: (0.000, 0.000) Manim
- Pixel Bounding Box: x = 0, y = 0, w = 1920 px, h = 1080 px
- Presentation Safe Zone: Minimal overlay title only; teacher occupies full frame.

B. LAYOUT 3: SME 70% — SPLIT LEFT SME + RIGHT CONTENT AREA (guidelines.md Section 8.5)
- Left Blue Accent Band:
  * Color: #2C81FE (or #1A73E8)
  * Dimensions: x = 0 to 242 px, full height (y = 0 to 1080 px)
- Cropped SME Frame:
  * Position: x = 80 px (or 63–129 px), y = 80 px (or 182–205 px)
  * Dimensions: Width = 640 px (625–667 px), Height = 920 px (722–734 px)
  * Mandatory Corner Radius: 10 px (0.074 Manim units)
- Safe Right Content Area:
  * Horizontal Bounds: x_min = 760 px (minimum 40px margin from SME frame) to x_max = 1840 px
  * Vertical Bounds: y_min = 80 px to y_max = 1000 px
  * Content Card (Slide 19 example): 1022 × 546 px at x = 793 px, y = 224 px
- Collision Rule: Under NO circumstances may any slide text, card, or diagram cross x < 760 px.

C. LAYOUT 2: SME 20% — TOP-RIGHT PIP + FULL CONTENT (guidelines.md Section 8.4)
- 20% SME PIP Box:
  * Position: Top-Right Corner (x = 1536 px, y = 0 px flush, or x = 1496 px, y = 40 px)
  * Dimensions: Width = 384 px, Height = 216 px (20% of FHD)
  * Corner Radius: 10 px (0.074 Manim units)
- Top-Left Title Pill: x = 0, y = 28 px, 277 × 74 px
- Lead-In Line Box: x = 439 px, y = 125 px, width = 1098 px, height = 140 px
- CRITICAL COLLISION RULE (Slide 27):
  * The lead-in line and any header text MUST terminate at or left of x = 1450 px
    (Manim x <= +4.267, 80% of canvas width). It must NEVER enter the 20% SME window.
- Main Diagram Zone:
  * Bounding Area: x = 52 px to 1814 px, y = 270 px to 1034 px
  * CRITICAL VERTICAL CLEARANCE: Main diagrams, tables, and code blocks must stay below
    y = 270 px (Manim y <= +2.000) directly under the PIP window.

4. EDITORIAL & ON-SCREEN TEXT (OST) STANDARDS (guidelines.md Section 4)
- Titles & Headings:
  * Casing: Chicago Manual of Style (Title Casing).
  * Word Count: 5 to 7 words maximum. Avoid one-word titles.
  * Question Titles: Allowed sparingly, but avoid starting with "Why"
    (e.g., "Why Is Sales Forecasting Necessary?" -> "Need for Sales Forecasting").
  * Demos & Tool Slides: Start with an "-ing" verb (e.g., "Analyzing Performance Data").
- Lead-In Lines & Bullets:
  * Active voice, US English, simple present tense.
  * No conversational tone in OST (e.g., avoid "Now, let's see an example.").
  * Avoid abbreviations & contractions (e.g., avoid "e.g.", "i.e.", "&", "etc.", "don't").
  * Numbers Rule: Spell out numbers 0 to 9 in words ("zero" through "nine").
  * Punctuation Rule: Complete sentences MUST end with a period (.). Fragments, bulleted
    phrases, and incomplete statements MUST NOT have periods.
  * Emphasis: Never use single or double quotes for emphasis; use bold or colored highlights.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ATTENTION-GUIDING HIGHLIGHTS (guidelines.md Section 3.3)
- Focus Stroke Color: #F78908 (Vibrant Orange).
- Stroke Thickness: Exactly 3 px (0.022 Manim units).
- Draw Animation Time: Exactly 1.0 second animated reveal.
- Rule: Highlight only ONE element at a time when the SME speaks about it. Mute others.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. VISUAL ASSET RESTRICTIONS (guidelines.md Section 6.2)
- DO NOT use 3D images or 3D models.
- DO NOT use dotted lines, silhouette images, or black-and-white graphics.
- DO NOT use thick outlines or drop shadows.
- All shapes must use light pastel colors with #404040 text inside.
- All cards and shapes must use rounded edges only (10 px corner radius).
