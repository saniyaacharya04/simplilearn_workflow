Presentation Step 1

Issue: Sockets overlap with the central Agent Core, their labels overflow their containers or are hidden, and the Memory socket is positioned incorrectly.

Suggestions:

*   Increase the `orbit_radius` value in the scene setup to 3.5 units to provide more clearance between `agent_core` and the `sockets_grp`.
*   Adjust the Y-coordinate calculation for `pos_south` and `pos_north` to use the full `orbit_radius` instead of `orbit_radius * 0.88` to maintain consistent radial spacing.
*   Reposition `sock_s` from its current South position to the South-East. Shift `sock_s` RIGHT by `orbit_radius * 0.707` units and DOWN by `orbit_radius * 0.707` units (relative to `center_pos`), ensuring it remains on the adjusted orbital circumference.
*   Increase the width of the `RoundedRectangle` background for `sock_n` (Reasoning Engine) to accommodate its text label with a minimum of 0.25 units padding on each side.
*   Increase the width of the `RoundedRectangle` background for `sock_s` (Memory Layer) to accommodate its text label with a minimum of 0.25 units padding on each side.
*   Ensure that the `txt` (text label) within each socket (e.g., `sock_n`, `sock_s`, `sock_e`, `sock_w`) is rendered above its `bg` (RoundedRectangle background) by setting a higher z_index for the text or its containing `content` VGroup within the `make_socket` function.
