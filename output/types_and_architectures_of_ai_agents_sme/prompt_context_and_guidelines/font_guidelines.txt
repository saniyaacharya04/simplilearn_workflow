# Simplilearn Typography & Editorial Guidelines (OSL 3.0 & Blended Learning Standards)
Derived Strictly from guidelines.md (Sections 4.2, 4.3, 5.1, 6.3, 8.1)

## 1. Typography Hierarchy
- Font Family: **Open Sans** exclusively across all presentation scenes and assets.
- Splash Screen / Topic Title: Open Sans Bold, 45 px (0.333 Manim units), Color #FFFFFF.
- Splash Screen Subtitle: Open Sans Regular, 45 px, Color #FFFFFF.
- Content Screen Body / Bullets: Open Sans Regular, 34 px (0.252 Manim units), Color #404040.
- Section / Card Headers: Open Sans Bold, 24-28 px, Color #404040 or #1E293B (Navy Blue is prohibited).
- Primary Content Text Color: Always #404040 on light cards or white canvas. Never use white text on light backgrounds.

## 2. Slide Titles & Headings (Chicago Manual of Style)
- Word Count: Maximum 5–7 words (avoid one-word titles).
- Casing: Title Case per Chicago Manual of Style.
- Avoid starting question titles with "Why" (e.g. "Need for Sales Forecasting" instead of "Why Is Sales Forecasting Necessary?").
- Demos / activities: Start with an "-ing" verb (e.g. "Analyzing Feedback Using GenAI Tools").
- Punctuation: Colon (:) separates title from subtitle; question mark (?) for question titles.

## 3. Editorial & On-Screen Text (OST) Standards
- Language & Grammar: **US English**, grammatically correct, active voice, simple present / present indefinite tense.
- Pronunciation standard: Merriam-Webster.
- Periods & Casing: Complete sentences MUST end with a period (.); incomplete sentences, fragments, and bullet points must NOT end with a period.
- No Title Redundancy: Do not start the lead-in line with the slide title.
- No Bottom-Line Redundancy: Bottom line must not repeat content already in lead-in or diagram.
- Convert Sentences to Anchors: Distill spoken sentences into keywords or short phrases. Never duplicate spoken sentences verbatim.
- Numbers: Spell out numbers 0–9 in narrative text ("two numeric variables", not "2"; "four missing values", not "4").
- Dates: US English date format MM/DD/YYYY consistently.
- Highlighting Text: Do NOT use single quotes ('...') or double quotes ("...") to emphasize text—use bold or italics instead.
- Prohibited Forms: Avoid contractions ("do not" instead of "don't") and abbreviations (avoid "e.g.", "i.e.", "&", "etc.").
- Tone: Non-conversational for lead-in and bottom lines (avoid "I", "we", "me", "Now let's see..."). Second-person ("you") is permitted.
- End-of-video consistent format: "In the next topic, you will learn about: ..." or "In the next topic, you will learn how to: ...".
